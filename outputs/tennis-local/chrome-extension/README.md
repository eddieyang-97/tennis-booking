# Tennis booking extension — v0.6.5 MVP

Runs in normal Chrome using your Courtside sign-in. It checks and books at London midnight, seven days before the selected playing date, then attempts payment. Any playing day; starts at 20:00 or 21:00. Bethnal Green Gardens precedes Victoria Park; two hours precede one, with consecutive hours in the same venue; switching courts is allowed, with same-court options tried first. Limits are £14 for two hours and £7 for one. Confirmed dates and unresolved checkouts block duplicate bookings. Each armed job runs once.

## Setup and scheduling

v0.6.0 fixes the rapid two-hour selection sequence. It validates all requested hours, then clicks one hour at a time, waits for a stable checkbox and basket-count acknowledgement, and resolves the next checkbox afresh after site updates. It never repeats an uncertain click. Basket reads also require stable snapshots, and failures report expected versus actual items and per-hour identity. Live testing of this sequence is still required.

v0.5.9 closes the remaining scheduled account/basket timeout gap: read-only account, basket and purchase-history checks wait up to 60 seconds and recognise ready DOMs without waiting for the whole tab to finish loading. Scheduled card-page/post-fill readiness also waits up to 60 seconds. Manual checks retain shorter budgets. Failed reads retain diagnostic tabs; changed payment identity, unexpected destinations and real authentication requirements stop. Nothing in this update retries card filling or payment. Exported status now includes the installed version.

The midnight grid policy uses a 60-second budget per attempt and waits five seconds before one fresh retry. This applies both to the scheduled availability scan and its pre-selection grid read. Manual checks retain 20-second attempts and a 500 ms pause. A ready grid can be read before the whole page finishes loading. The scheduled scan proceeds immediately when the preferred venue has an eligible candidate; manual checks still inspect both venues. Sign-in/human verification and changed layouts stop without retries. Final failure retains a diagnostic tab and reports availability as unknown; no payment or selection is blindly retried. The extended budgets have not yet been validated under a real midnight traffic surge.

1. Load this directory as an unpacked Chrome extension, or reload after updating. Reload any existing checkout tab to attach its scripts.
2. Open **Set up or unlock card file**. Enter card number, expiry, CVC and billing postcode locally; choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters. Creation downloads `tennis-card.encrypted.json` and unlocks it for the current Chrome session.
3. Close setup, choose a future playing date, and use **Arm midnight booking and payment**. Unlock the existing file before subsequent sessions. Keep Chrome open and the laptop awake. Chrome restart or extension reload locks the card.

The playing-date field defaults to London’s today plus eight days. It does not skip weekends automatically or change an already armed job; choose the desired playing date before arming. The armed response shows the saved date and release in UTC. During BST, 23:00Z means midnight London on the next calendar day. Preflight runs five minutes before release. An armed response does not mean preflight has passed or a booking has succeeded.

After a source update, reload the extension once, then unlock the card again. The saved schedule survives reload, but the unlocked card does not. Keep the card unlocked and avoid another reload before the run.

The file uses AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a random salt and IV. Card details stay only in trusted extension session storage while unlocked; they are not written to project files, persistent extension storage, logs or results. Store the encrypted file separately from the extension source.

Payments require an unlocked local card and fill Stripe's card form directly. There is no Link fallback. The popup shows whether the card is unlocked. Unlock verifies the session-storage write. Arming, preflight and release check card availability before proceeding. Receipt-only recovery does not require an unlocked card. Human verification and bank approval can still require intervention.

## Recovery and historical payment test

A stopped selection remains pending deliberately, so a lost response cannot cause another selection/payment. Recovery records now include the observed basket and selection progress. To abandon an incomplete selection or an unpaid prepared basket, manually remove its items on the site, then use **Reconcile cleared selection**. The extension only archives a pre-checkout record after a stable empty-basket check; it removes no site items and cannot clear any provisional reference or dispatched payment. Historical archived selections appear in exported status as `abandonedCheckouts`.

**Check and recover** contains availability checks, site-verification resume, checkout preparation, payment reconciliation and paid-receipt verification. The availability reset does not erase payment uncertainty. The legacy basket reconciliation control only verifies an explicitly empty basket; it removes nothing and exists for unresolved records from older versions.

The paid-test button and its launch action were removed in v0.5.2. The earlier £2.50 test remains in historical payment records; receipt reconciliation still supports that record without submitting another charge. Do not delete its submission marker. This update does not change saved jobs, but reloading the extension clears the unlocked card session; unlock again before an armed run.

The extension verifies slot, total and provisional reference, commits its payment journal before submitting once, and requires matching paid evidence. It now reconciles the merchant account purchase history if the receipt tab is closed, stale or unrecognised, requiring the exact dispatched reference and amount in one Confirmed / Paid ✔ row. Account history confirms payment and leaves active booking state unknown. An interrupted or uncertain submission is never charged again automatically. Unknown layouts and mismatched details stop for inspection. Discovery and temporary read retries are bounded; run steps include timestamps. Arming, preflight and release reconcile an older dispatched payment before proceeding; unpaid unresolved baskets still stop.

Existing availability-only or basket-only jobs retain their saved modes; updating does not convert them into paid jobs or erase journals. Chrome alarms cannot wake a sleeping laptop. Checks more than a minute late stop.

## Card file troubleshooting

After an extension reload, close old card-setup tabs and reopen setup from the popup. Select the original `tennis-card.encrypted.json` and enter its original passphrase exactly, including spaces. The file format is unchanged; existing files remain supported. The setup now distinguishes invalid JSON, unsupported or damaged encryption fields, decryption failure, and failures storing a decrypted card in browser memory. A decryption authentication failure can mean either an incorrect passphrase or changed ciphertext; these cannot be distinguished reliably. Error messages contain no entered values or underlying browser error contents. JSON files with a UTF-8 byte-order mark are supported. The maintained suite has 72 passing tests.


Before payment submission, a reloaded checkout may issue a new provisional reference. The extension accepts that replacement only after re-verifying the exact basket and amount, and records the prior reference with a timestamp. Once payment dispatch is journalled, its persistent submission marker prevents another payment even if other phase metadata changes. Checkout preparation cannot erase that marker or a legacy uncertain-payment phase; only a matching paid receipt can confirm the record. Unknown phases stop for inspection.

Reload the extension before unlocking the card, not after. Once setup confirms the card is unlocked, close setup and reopen the popup on the checkout tab. Confirm the top line says Local card: unlocked, then run the test. Receipt reconciliation remains available without card access and cannot submit another charge.


Stripe's empty Card Element initially contains number, expiry and CVC inputs but no postcode input. Autofill validates those primary fields, enters the number, then resolves each remaining visible input afresh and waits up to five seconds per field for it to appear. Missing, disabled, read-only or ambiguous fields stop without payment. Failure messages identify the stage without returning field values. The collapsed-form sequence and missing-postcode stop are covered by regression tests.

### v0.6.3: read-only connection recovery and diagnostics

Basket reading reported `PAGE_TAB_ERROR` while the expected page was still loading; the old log hid the underlying error, so its precise cause is unconfirmed. Read-only polling now tolerates Chrome's asynchronous message-channel closure and removed-frame errors within the existing deadline, without reloading or repeating selection/payment. Reload v0.6.3, ensure the test basket is manually empty, then use **Reconcile cleared selection**; this rechecks the empty basket without another selection. ### v0.6.4: prioritize one verified hour and preserve debugging context

Scheduled checkout can now downgrade an intended two-hour selection to one hour only when the stable basket contains exactly one of its intended venue/date/hour/court items, with an approved 20:00 or 21:00 start, exact item/total price at most £7, and the known payment boundary. No extra selection/removal is sent. The journal must still be `selection-dispatched`, without a provisional reference or payment dispatch marker; other pending/confirmed-date guards remain. An unrelated item, wrong total, 22:00 start, uncertain read, or extra item stops the flow. Manual preparation and daytime/legacy paid tests do not enable this policy. Payment re-reads and validates the downgraded basket before opening checkout, so a late second item causes a stop rather than an unwanted purchase.

The converted journal retains `fallbackFrom` (original slot/key, expected/actual basket evidence, timestamp); the new one-hour identity is committed before proceeding and survives restart. Runs now record extension version, retain 100 runs of up to 100 timestamped steps each, and checkout journals include `preparationRunId` for correlation with payment runs. This is bounded local logging, not remote monitoring or an indefinite audit archive; copy the status JSON after a failure before many later checks rotate it out. Logs omit card fields, passphrases and page HTML. The one-hour fallback has regression coverage including mixed courts, either intended hour, unsafe/overpriced baskets, no repeated selection and protected payment journals; live fallback remains unverified.

Remaining robustness limits: only the first eligible candidate is prepared; automatic safe re-search before any selection dispatch is a future improvement. A human verification page, expired session, locked card, bank challenge, closed Chrome or sleeping laptop can still stop unattended operation. Paid receipt recovery establishes payment and does not necessarily prove an active reservation (the account purchase table can still show a canceled purchase). Do not retry Pay after an uncertain submission. A successful real midnight run is still outstanding.

### v0.6.5: explicitly armed dates can be any weekday

Removed the Monday–Thursday restriction for normal availability checks, arming, candidate selection, checkout and payment recovery. Friday, Saturday and Sunday now use the same 20:00/21:00 start preferences, duration/court rules and £14/£7 limits. Each job remains explicitly armed for one playing date and runs once at London midnight seven days before play; the popup still defaults to today plus eight days. Date validity, booking-window checks, future release requirement, pending journals and duplicate-date protection remain in force. Historical weekday-limited policy descriptions earlier in these notes are superseded by this update.
