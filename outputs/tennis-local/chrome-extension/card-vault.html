Local tennis payment card

Enter your details here on your laptop. The downloaded file is encrypted with your passphrase. Unlock it before the midnight run; the card stays in this extension's browser memory until you lock it or Chrome restarts. No passphrase or card data is saved in logs.

Use an existing card file

Payment uses the card form directly, without Link. Your bank can still require authentication. Only the authorised court booking and exact price limits can be paid.
